# A note for the SwN team: support, supervision and what's changed

Hello everyone,

We've made some changes to the Strolling with Neurokin website about **support and
supervision** — what we can offer on a stroll, and what we can't. This note explains what's
changed and what it means for you on the day.

Nothing here changes what you already do brilliantly. It mostly puts in writing something
that has always been true, so that nobody arrives expecting more than we're able to give.

---

## Why we've done this

Our volunteers are walk leaders and hosts. We are **not** carers, and we're not set up to
provide personal care, one-to-one support or supervision.

Until now, that wasn't actually said anywhere on the website. The only place it came up was
a question buried in the registration form — which meant somebody could sign up, turn up,
and only then discover we couldn't offer the support they needed. That isn't fair on them,
and it isn't fair on you.

So we've said it clearly, in plain language, in the places people actually look.

---

## What's changed for people signing up

**On the homepage and the booking page**, there's now a short, friendly explanation that:

- our volunteers are walk leaders and hosts, **not carers**
- we can't provide personal care, one-to-one support or supervision
- if someone needs that kind of support, they need to **bring someone with them who
  provides it** — a carer, support worker, family member or friend
- they shouldn't attend on their own if they need that level of support, because we aren't
  equipped for it and it isn't safe for them

**On the registration form**, the "are you attending with anyone else?" question is now a
simple **Yes / No**. If someone answers **Yes**, a few more questions appear:

- the **full name and relationship** of everyone coming with them
- whether one of them is a **carer or support worker** — and if so, that person's
  **full name, agency/organisation, and mobile number**
- **who is responsible for supervising whom**, especially where someone is under 18, has
  higher support needs, may be vulnerable, or needs active supervision
- a **tick box** confirming they've understood that parents, carers and support workers stay
  responsible for the person they attend with

Anyone answering **No** just sees the same short form as before — we haven't made life
harder for the majority.

We've also been upfront that not telling us about relevant supervision needs may affect
whether someone can attend future walks. That isn't meant as a threat; it's so we can plan
properly and keep everyone safe.

---

## What this means for you on the day

**Before the walk**, open the walk's registrations in the admin area and look at the
**"Attending With"** column. You'll now see:

- who is coming with someone else, and who those people are
- any carer or support worker's name, organisation and **mobile number**
- who has said they're responsible for whom

**Please save those mobile numbers to your phone before you set off.** If you need to find
someone's carer during the walk, you don't want to be logging into a website to do it.

**In the moment**, if someone seems to need more support than we can offer:

- You are a host, not a carer. You are **not** expected to provide personal care, one-to-one
  support or supervision.
- You are also **not expected to judge** whether someone needs a carer. That isn't your call
  to make, and nobody expects you to make it.
- Do what feels reasonable and kind, keep yourself and everyone else safe, and **don't take
  on more than you're comfortable with**.
- You're never alone — there are always two of you. One can stay with the group while the
  other helps.
- If the person came with a carer or support worker, **go to them first**. Supervision is
  their responsibility, not yours.
- If anyone is in danger or needs urgent medical help, **call 999**.

**Afterwards**, write down what actually happened as soon as you can — what you *saw and
did*, not conclusions about the person. Then **tell Chris**, who will follow it up properly.

A reminder of this is now at the top of the admin dashboard, so it's there when you need it
rather than filed away in a document nobody can find.

---

## The bit that matters most

If someone turns up needing more support than we can give, that is **not a failure on your
part**, and it is **not yours to fix on the day**. Everyone is told before they book what we
can and can't offer. You are never obliged to fill that gap.

Do what's kind and reasonable, keep everyone safe, and pass it on to Chris afterwards.

Thank you — genuinely — for everything you do on these walks. This is about protecting you
as much as anyone.

Chris
