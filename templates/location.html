Volunteer Support

Every stroll is supported by at least two London Autism Group Charity volunteers alongside your walk leader. They're there to welcome you, help, and answer questions along the way.

ℹ️ Before you book

Our volunteers are friendly walk leaders and hosts, not carers. They aren't able to provide personal care, one-to-one support, or supervision.

If you need that kind of support during the walk, please bring someone with you who provides it — a carer, support worker, family member or friend. Please don't attend on your own if you need that level of support: we aren't equipped for it, and it isn't safe for you.

Just let us know below so we can plan numbers and be ready for you both.
